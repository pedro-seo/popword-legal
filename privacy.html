Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Summary: POPWORD does not ask for an email, a password or other contact details. It does process pseudonymous identifiers, a public nickname, scores and, only if you opt in, usage analytics. On first launch you can optionally choose a public nickname and accept the Terms of Use. An anonymous account is created if you save that nickname, open the full leaderboard, submit a score or send a report. You can remove the public nickname, scores and reports from the game. The app shows no ads and does not sell data.

Data controller

Palo Seco SL (Barcelona, Spain), publishing the game as DRYWOOD GAMES. Contact: pedro@palo-seco.com.

What data we process and why

1. Online leaderboard (Supabase). Saving a public nickname, opening the full leaderboard, submitting a score or sending a report creates an account with a random user id and no email or password (Supabase anonymous sign-in). That id is a pseudonymous identifier. With it we store the public nickname you choose and the daily and all-time scores you submit. The nickname and scores are public on the leaderboard. Purpose: running the leaderboard. Choose a nickname that does not identify you.

2. Reports and blocks. If you report another player's nickname, we insert a report (your anonymous id, the reported id and nickname, the reason and the date). A report is insert-only: the game has no control to edit or withdraw that single report. Other players cannot see your reports. A nickname is hidden when 3 distinct people have reported it. Players you block are stored only on your device, and the block hides them on that device. This policy does not promise a review within a fixed time.

3. Usage analytics (PostHog), only if you opt in. If you accept analytics in the in-game prompt or in Settings, we send gameplay events (app start, levels played, scores, shop use with in-game coins, settings changes) and error messages. They are tied to a device identifier (distinct_id), separate from the Supabase user id. The app does not send the Supabase user id to PostHog. We do not send the words you form or your nickname. We use the events to balance the game and fix bugs. If you do not opt in, nothing is sent to PostHog. Turning analytics off stops further events from the app. The app does not call PostHog to delete events already sent.

4. Data stored on your device. Your progress, achievements, dictionary, coins, settings, blocked players and your analytics choice stay on your device. Settings → Erase data is a local reset: it clears that on-device progress and replaces the local session and the device identifier. It does not remove the public nickname, scores or reports, and it does not call PostHog. Settings has no save code and no export or import. Uninstalling the app can delete the local progress. Word lists, Spanish definitions and fonts are credited in the Terms of Use.

Legal basis

The leaderboard and reports are based on performing the Terms of Use you accept to take part (GDPR art. 6.1.b) and on our legitimate interest in keeping the leaderboard free of offensive content (art. 6.1.f). Analytics are based on your consent (art. 6.1.a), which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.

Retention

This policy does not set a fixed retention period for nicknames, scores, reports or analytics, and it does not confirm the providers' retention settings. You can remove the public nickname, scores and reports as described below. Hiding a nickname after reports from 3 distinct people stops it being shown; that hide is separate from deletion. Replacing the device identifier does not delete analytics events already sent under the previous identifier.

Processors

Supabase processes the anonymous account, the public nickname, scores and reports. PostHog processes analytics events only if you opt in. This policy does not confirm that either provider stores the data in the European Union, that IP-address capture is disabled, or that a particular retention setting applies. We do not sell data. We do not pass it to anyone else except these providers, or where the law requires it.

Advertising

POPWORD shows no ads, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not sell data.

Your choices

  • Delete your leaderboard data in the game: Settings → Delete my leaderboard data. This removes the public nickname, scores and reports, then attempts to delete the anonymous authentication user. Deleting that authentication user can fail even when the public nickname, scores and reports have been removed.
  • Local reset: Settings → Erase data clears on-device progress, achievements and settings, and replaces the local session and the device identifier. It does not call PostHog to delete events already sent. There is no save code to copy to another device. Uninstalling the app can delete local progress.
  • Withdraw analytics consent: Settings → turn off Anonymous analytics. New events stop. The app does not call PostHog to delete events already sent. You can email pedro@palo-seco.com to ask about those events.
  • Skip the anonymous account: you can play without saving a nickname, opening the full leaderboard, submitting a score or sending a report. Those actions create the anonymous account and need a connection.
  • You can also request deletion by email at pedro@palo-seco.com, including your nickname.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection by writing to pedro@palo-seco.com. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or your local authority.

Children

POPWORD does not ask for an email, a password or other contact details. It does process pseudonymous identifiers, a public nickname, scores and, if you opt in, analytics. If you are under 14 (or the age of digital consent in your country), do not choose a nickname or accept analytics without a parent's or guardian's permission. A guardian can request deletion at pedro@palo-seco.com. The same limits as in Your choices apply, including that deletion of the authentication user can fail after the public rows are removed.

Changes

If the way we handle data changes, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before releasing the change.
