Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Summary: POPWORD has no sign-up: we never ask for an email, password, name, phone number or location. For the online leaderboard an anonymous account is created automatically. We store the nickname and scores you send to the leaderboard, and you can delete them yourself in the game. Analytics run only if you opt in. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell data.

Data controller

Palo Seco SL (Barcelona, Spain), publishing the game as DRYWOOD GAMES. Contact: pedro@palo-seco.com.

What data we process and why

1. Online leaderboard (Supabase, EU servers). The first time you open the leaderboard or send a score, the game creates an anonymous account with a random identifier (user ID), with no email or password. With that identifier we store the nickname you choose (or an automatic one such as "Tinta-1A2B") and your daily and all-time leaderboard scores. Nickname and scores are public on the leaderboard. Purpose: running the leaderboard. Please use a nickname that does not identify you.

2. Reports and blocks. If you report another player's nickname, we store the report (your anonymous identifier, the reported identifier and nickname, the reason and the date) to review it. Nobody else can see your reports. Players you block are stored only on your device.

3. Usage analytics (PostHog, EU servers), only if you opt in. If you accept analytics in the in-game prompt or in Settings, we send gameplay events (app start, levels played, scores, shop use with in-game coins, settings changes) and error messages, tied to a random identifier that is different from the leaderboard one. We never send the words you form or your nickname. We use them only to balance the game and fix bugs. If you do not opt in, nothing is sent to PostHog.

4. Data stored on your device. Your progress, achievements, dictionary, coins, settings, blocked players and your analytics choice stay on your device.

Legal basis

The leaderboard and reports are based on performing the Terms of Use you accept to take part (GDPR art. 6.1.b) and on our legitimate interest in keeping the leaderboard free of offensive content (art. 6.1.f). Analytics are based on your consent (art. 6.1.a), which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.

Retention

Nicknames and scores are kept while the leaderboard is active or until you delete them. Reports are kept for up to 12 months. Analytics events are kept for at most 12 months.

Processors

Supabase and PostHog act as data processors under data processing agreements and host the data in the European Union. We do not share data with third parties unless required by law.

Advertising and tracking

POPWORD shows no ads, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and never sells or shares data.

Your choices

  • Delete your leaderboard data in the game: Settings → Delete my leaderboard data. Your nickname, scores, reports and anonymous account are deleted from our servers immediately.
  • Erase everything: Settings → Erase data also removes progress, achievements and settings from the device.
  • Withdraw analytics consent: Settings → turn off Anonymous analytics. If you also want us to delete events already sent, email us.
  • Skip the leaderboard: you can play offline and never open the leaderboard.
  • You can also request deletion by email at pedro@palo-seco.com, including your nickname.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection by writing to pedro@palo-seco.com. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or your local authority.

Children

POPWORD does not ask for personal data. If you are under 14 (or the age of digital consent in your country), do not choose a nickname or accept analytics without a parent's or guardian's permission. We delete data on a guardian's request.

Changes

If the way we handle data changes, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before releasing the change.
