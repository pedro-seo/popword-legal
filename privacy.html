Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Summary: POPWORD needs no account or sign-up. If you join the leaderboard, we store your nickname and scores. Analytics run only if you opt in. There are no ads, no tracking for advertising, and we never sell data.

Data controller

Palo Seco SL (Barcelona, Spain), publishing the game as DRYWOOD GAMES. Contact: pj.serranorivera@gmail.com.

What data we process and why

1. Online leaderboard (Supabase, EU servers, Frankfurt). If you submit a score to the leaderboard, we store the nickname you choose, a random anonymous ID generated on your device, and your scores (runs, Daily POPWORD and streaks). Your nickname and scores are public on the leaderboard. We don't ask for your name, email, phone number or location. Please use a nickname that doesn't identify you.

2. Usage analytics (PostHog, EU servers), only if you opt in. If you accept analytics in the in-game prompt or in Settings, we send usage events: app opens, runs, rounds, purchases in the in-game shop (made with game currency, not real money), errors and basic technical data (device model, OS, language and app version), tied to a random ID. We use them only to understand how the game is played and to improve balance and stability. If you don't opt in, nothing is sent to PostHog.

3. Data stored on your device. Your progress, achievements, streak, settings and analytics choice are stored only on your device.

Legal basis

We process this data based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You give it by submitting your nickname and scores to the leaderboard and by opting in to analytics. You can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawing doesn't affect processing that has already taken place.

Retention

Nicknames and scores are kept while the leaderboard is active or until you ask us to delete them. We delete them within 30 days of your request. Analytics events are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted.

Processors and transfers

Supabase and PostHog act as data processors under data processing agreements and host the data in the European Union. We don't share data with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Advertising and tracking

POPWORD shows no ads. It doesn't use your data to track you across other companies' apps or websites, doesn't use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and never sells or shares data for advertising.

Your choices

  • Revoke analytics: turn analytics off in the game's Settings. From that moment, no more events are sent. Email us if you also want the events already sent to be deleted.
  • Skip the leaderboard: you can play without ever submitting a nickname or a score.
  • Delete your nickname and scores: email pj.serranorivera@gmail.com with the subject "POPWORD - Delete my data" and tell us your nickname. If you have it handy, also include your player ID from Settings. We delete them within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Delete local data: delete the app from your device.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, erase, restrict or port your data, or object to its processing, by emailing pj.serranorivera@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or to your local authority.

Children

POPWORD doesn't ask for personal data. If you are under 14 (or under the age of digital consent where you live), don't submit a nickname or opt in to analytics without a parent's or guardian's permission. If a guardian asks us to, we will delete the data.

Changes

If the way we handle data changes, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before releasing the change.
