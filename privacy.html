Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Summary: POPWORD is a single-player game. It does not ask for an account, an email, a password or other contact details. Your game is stored only on your device. Usage analytics (PostHog, in the EU) are sent only if you opt in. If you decline, nothing is sent. The app shows no ads and does not sell data.

Data controller

Palo Seco SL (Barcelona, Spain), publishing the game as DRYWOOD GAMES. Contact: pedro@palo-seco.com.

What data we process and why

1. Data stored on your device. The app stores the following only on the device, in local storage (localStorage):

  • Game progress: stars, best scores per level, coins, boosters, jokers, chests, daily best, streak, calendar, achievements, the dictionary of words you have discovered, and stats.
  • Settings: language, audio, accessibility, and your analytics choice.
  • A random player identifier (a UUID created on the device). It is not an account and it is not your name. It stays on the device, and it is sent only if you opt in to analytics, as the analytics identifier.

Settings → Erase data deletes all of it on the device. There is no export or import. Uninstalling the app can delete it. Word lists, Spanish definitions and fonts are credited in the Terms of Use.

2. Usage analytics (PostHog), only if you opt in. PostHog is used in the EU, at eu.i.posthog.com. The app sends analytics only after you opt in on the consent prompt. You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings. If you decline, nothing is sent.

If you opt in, the app sends gameplay events: level start, level clear and level fail; stars; score; boosters; jokers; tutorial steps; the daily puzzle; settings changes; and the length of a word and a score bucket. It never sends the words you form. It sends the random player UUID as the identifier, and short error messages of at most 180 characters. It does not send your name, your email or those words. PostHog can see the IP address of the connection. We use the events to understand how the game is played and to fix bugs. Turning analytics off stops further events. Erasing data on the device does not delete events PostHog has already received, and the app does not delete them.

3. Links. Links to these legal pages and to pedro@palo-seco.com open only when you tap them. The app does not open them on its own.

Legal basis

Storing progress, settings and the random player UUID on your device is necessary to provide the game (GDPR art. 6.1.b). Analytics are based on your consent (art. 6.1.a). You can withdraw that consent at any time in Settings. Withdrawing consent stops new events. It does not delete events PostHog has already received.

Retention

On-device data stays until you delete it with Settings → Erase data, or until it is removed with the app. This policy does not set a fixed retention period for analytics events already received by PostHog. Turning analytics off, or erasing data on the device, does not delete those events.

Processors

If you opt in, PostHog processes the analytics events described above. Those events are sent to PostHog in the European Union (eu.i.posthog.com). PostHog can see the IP address of the connection. We do not sell data. We do not pass data to anyone else except PostHog when you have opted in, or where the law requires it.

Advertising

POPWORD shows no ads, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not sell data.

Your choices

  • Erase on-device data: Settings → Erase data deletes the progress, settings and random player UUID stored on the device.
  • Analytics: If you decline the consent prompt, nothing is sent. If you opt in, you can turn analytics off at any time in Settings. New events stop. The app does not delete events PostHog has already received.
  • Links: Legal pages and pedro@palo-seco.com open only when you tap them.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection by writing to pedro@palo-seco.com. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or your local authority.

Children

POPWORD does not ask for an account, an email, a password or other contact details. Analytics are sent only if you opt in. If you are under 14 (or the age of digital consent in your country), do not accept analytics without a parent's or guardian's permission. A guardian can write to pedro@palo-seco.com.

Changes

If the way we handle data changes, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before releasing the change.
