Support

Questions, a bug, an idea, or a word that should count but doesn't? Email pedro@palo-seco.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and, for a word, the dictionary language. We reply as soon as we can.

FAQ

How do I play? Swipe across neighbouring letters, diagonals included, to make words of 3+ letters. Each level is a 60-second round: reach the target score before time runs out to earn up to three stars. Longer words score more and add bonus seconds.

When does the Daily POPWORD reset? Every day at 00:00 Madrid time (CET/CEST). Everyone in the world gets the same board.

Do I need an account or internet? No account needed, and you can play offline. Only the online leaderboard needs a connection.

Are there ads or purchases? No. Version 1.0 has no ads and no in-app purchases.

How do I change the dictionary language? In Settings, choose English or Spanish (with Ñ).

How do I delete my leaderboard data? In Settings, tap Delete my leaderboard data. Your nickname, scores and anonymous account are deleted from our servers right away.

I saw an offensive nickname. On the leaderboard, tap ⋯ next to it and choose Report nickname or Block player. We review every report within 24 hours. You can also email us the nickname. See the Terms of Use.

How do I turn off analytics? In Settings. See Your privacy choices for more.

Is my progress kept if I change iPhone? Progress is stored on the device. To move it, copy your save code in Settings → Save and import it on the new device. If you delete the app without a backup or save code, it's lost.

How do I turn off sound or haptics? In Settings you can switch music, sound effects and haptics on or off.
