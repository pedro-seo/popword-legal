Support

Questions, a bug, an idea, or a word that should count but doesn't? Email pedro@palo-seco.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and, for a word, the dictionary language.

FAQ

How do I play? Swipe across neighbouring letters, diagonals included, to make words of 3+ letters. Each level is a 60-second round: reach the target score before time runs out to earn up to three stars. Longer words score more and add bonus seconds.

When does the Daily POPWORD reset? Every day at 00:00 Madrid time (CET/CEST). Everyone in the world gets the same board.

Do I need an email account or internet? No account, no email and no password. You can play offline. If you opt in to analytics, those events are sent when the device is online. Links to the legal pages and to pedro@palo-seco.com open only when you tap them.

Are there ads or purchases? No. The app has no ads and no in-app purchases.

How do I change the dictionary language? In Settings, choose English or Spanish (with Ñ). Short definitions are shown only in Spanish. They come from Wiktionary, not from the Real Academia Española (RAE).

How do I erase my data? In Settings, tap Erase data. That deletes the progress, settings and random player UUID stored on this device. There is no export or import.

How do I turn off analytics? Analytics are sent only if you opt in on the consent prompt. If you decline, nothing is sent. In Settings you can turn analytics off at any time. Turning them off stops new events. The app does not delete events PostHog has already received. See Your privacy choices.

Is my progress kept if I change iPhone or delete the app? Progress, settings and a random player UUID are stored only on the device. There is no export or import. Erase data deletes all of that on the device. If you uninstall the app, or move to another iPhone, that on-device progress does not come with you.

Where do the word lists, definitions and fonts come from? See Third-party material in the Terms of Use.

How do I turn off sound or haptics? In Settings you can switch music, sound effects and haptics on or off.
