Support

Questions, a bug, an idea, or a word that should count but doesn't? Email pj.serranorivera@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and, for a word, the dictionary language. We reply as soon as we can.

FAQ

How do I play? Swipe across neighbouring letters, diagonals included, to make words of 3+ letters. Each word scores chips × multiplier. Hit the round's target score before you run out of plays.

When does the Daily POPWORD reset? Every day at 00:00 Madrid time (CET/CEST). Everyone in the world gets the same board.

Do I need an account or internet? No account needed, and you can play offline. Only the online leaderboard needs a connection.

Are there ads or purchases? No. Version 1.0 has no ads and no in-app purchases.

How do I change the dictionary language? In Settings, choose English or Spanish (with Ñ).

I want my nickname removed. Email us your nickname and we will delete it along with your scores. If you see an offensive nickname on the leaderboard, send us the nickname and the date and we will remove it.

How do I turn off analytics? In Settings. See Your privacy choices for more.

Is my progress kept if I change iPhone? Progress is stored on the device. If you delete the app or switch iPhones without restoring a backup, it's lost.

How do I turn off sound or haptics? In Settings you can switch music, sound effects and haptics on or off.
